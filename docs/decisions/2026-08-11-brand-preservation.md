# ADR: Brand preservation baseline

**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Status:** provisional pending owner approval  
**Inventory:** `docs/brand-inventory.md`

## Decision

Preserve the live dark/cyan retro-tech identity (Press Start 2P wordmark, Exo 2, Space Mono, `#00e5ff` on `#121212`, hard shadows, scanline atmosphere, category theme accents, NEW/POPULAR ribbons).

## Planned refinements (accessibility/performance — not a rebrand)

- Raise muted/line-strong and category-accent text colors to WCAG 2.2 AA on dark surfaces.
- Replace the current `/favicon.svg` (Cursor mark) with an approved Clarky3D mark.
- Self-host subset fonts; keep visual metrics close to live Google Fonts files.
- Provide no-JS HTML for catalogue essentials (plan requirement); visual polish may enhance after hydration.

## Requires explicit approval before shipping

- Any change to wordmark lettering, accent hue, or “Pep Things” public label.
- Use/reproduction rights for product photography and `og-default.jpg`.
- Introduction of light mode (not present live).
